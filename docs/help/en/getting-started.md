# Getting started

## Create your account

Sign up with your name, email and a password, and choose a name for your workspace. Your workspace
is your business — its products, stock and staff all live inside it, separate from every other
business using EzyCore.

After signing up you will see: **Account created successfully! Please check your email to verify your
account before logging in.** Open that email and click the verification link. You cannot sign in
until you do.

If the email does not arrive within a few minutes, check your spam folder. You can request another
from the login page.

## Sign in

Signing in asks for three things:

- **Organization Slug** — the short name of your workspace, like `abc-company`. It is in your
  welcome email.
- Your email
- Your password

Then press **Login**.

If you have forgotten your workspace name, use the "find your workspace" link and enter your email —
you will be taken to the right sign-in page. One email address can belong to several workspaces, so
if you help run more than one business you will be asked which one to open.

## Your dashboard

The dashboard is the first screen after signing in. It answers "what needs my attention today":

- **Quick Actions** — right under the greeting: big buttons for the screens you open every day
  (a new sale, your online orders) and shortcuts below them. A number on a shortcut means something
  is waiting, like orders to confirm or items running low. You only see shortcuts for the features
  your shop uses.
- **Low Stock Alerts** — items running low, so you can reorder before you run out.
- Sales and purchase trends, top selling items, and recent stock movements.

Most panels are empty on day one. They fill in as you record real sales and purchases.

## What to do next

Work through these in order. Each one builds on the last:

1. [Set up your shop](./set-up-your-shop.md) — your business details, locations and features.
2. [Add your products](./add-your-products.md) — what you sell.
3. [Add suppliers and customers](./add-contacts.md) — who you buy from and sell to.
4. [Receive stock](./receive-stock.md) — get your opening stock into the system.
5. [Make a sale](./make-a-sale.md).

## Switching language

EzyCore is available in English and Bangla. Change it from the menu in the top bar; the setting
follows your account, so it stays the same on your phone and your computer.
