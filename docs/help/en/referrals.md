# Referrals

Know another shopkeeper who could use EzyCore? **Settings → Referrals** gives you a link
to send them. The page says it plainly: *Share your referral link with other businesses.
When they sign up through it, you'll earn a commission on their subscription.*

## Your link

Under **Your referral link** you will find a web address that belongs only to you.
*Anyone who signs up through this link is credited to you.* — that is the whole mechanism.
There is nothing to set up and nothing to switch on.

Two ways to send it:

- **Copy** puts the link on your clipboard, ready to paste anywhere — SMS, Messenger,
  a Facebook post, an email.
- **Share on WhatsApp** opens WhatsApp with the link already written out, so you just pick
  who to send it to. This is how most referrals actually travel.

Your link never changes and never expires. Put it in your shop's Facebook bio if you like.

## How you get paid

When someone signs up through your link, their shop is permanently credited to you.
The commission is worked out from what they actually pay for their subscription, and it
keeps coming for as long as they stay a paying customer — it is not a one-off finder's fee.

Payouts are arranged directly with us. Contact support when you want to collect what you
have earned, and we will settle it with you.

## Things worth knowing

**They must use your link.** If a friend signs up on their own and mentions your name
afterwards, there is no record connecting you — the credit is created at the moment they
sign up, from the link they arrived through. Send the link.

**A shop can only be referred once.** Whoever's link they used first is the one credited,
permanently. Sending your link to someone already using EzyCore does nothing.

**Nothing changes for them.** They pay the normal price. Your commission comes from us,
not from their pocket, so there is nothing awkward to explain when you recommend it.

**If they stop paying, the commission stops.** It follows their subscription. If they come
back later, it resumes.

## If the page says your link isn't available

*Your referral link isn't available right now. Please try again in a moment.* usually means
the link is still being created for your shop, which happens shortly after signup. Wait a
few moments and reload. If it persists past a day, tell support — it is not something you
can fix from your side.
