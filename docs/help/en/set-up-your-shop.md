# Set up your shop

Do this once, before you add products. A few minutes here saves a lot of correcting later.

## Your business details

Under Settings → Organization, fill in your business name, address, phone and logo. These appear on
the invoices and receipts your customers receive, so use the details you want them to see.

## Your browser tab icon

The same page has a **Browser Tab Icon**. It is the small square shown in browser tabs and
bookmarks — both in the app you work in and in the shop your customers visit.

It is a separate upload from your logo on purpose, and your logo is never used in its place. A wide
logo squeezed into a tab-sized square comes out as an unreadable slice of itself, so put your full
logo above and a simple square mark here — an initial, a symbol, whatever stays recognisable when
it is tiny. Leave it empty and tabs show the EzyCore icon instead.

## Report emails

Daily summaries now live under **Settings → Notifications**, alongside every other message the
product sends — see [Notifications](notifications.md). You still choose whether each one goes out
and at what hour of your own day; they just share one page with order emails and alerts instead of
having a settings box of their own.

## Locations

A location is anywhere you hold stock — a shop, a warehouse, a storeroom. Every business starts with
one. Add more only if you genuinely keep stock in more than one place.

Locations matter more than they first appear:

- Stock counts are **per location**. A product can have 12 in the shop and 40 in the warehouse.
- Sales and purchases apply to whichever location you are working in. The location picker is in the
  top bar — check it before recording anything.
- Stock by Location shows every product across every location on one screen.

If you only ever have one location, you can safely ignore all of this — and the app hides it for
you. Stock by Location and Transfer Stock appear once **Multiple Locations** is switched on under
Customize workspace; with one location there is nothing to compare or transfer between.

## Turn features on and off

**Customize workspace** controls how much of EzyCore you see. It sits at the bottom of the sidebar,
always visible, so you can always find your way back to something you switched off. *Turn on only
the tools you need. You can change this any time — switching something off hides it, and never
deletes your data.*

Features worth deciding on early:

| Feature | Turn it on if |
|---|---|
| **Sales Management** | You sell from this system, not just track stock. Most businesses want this. |
| **Expiry Tracking** | You sell anything perishable — medicine, food, cosmetics. |
| **Ecommerce Storefront** | You want a public online shop as well as your counter. |
| VAT | You charge VAT on sales. |
| Returns Management | Customers return goods, or you return goods to suppliers. |
| Barcode System | You scan barcodes at the counter. |

**Expiry Tracking** deserves particular thought: it lets you *track product expiry dates with batch
management and alerts*, and it changes how stock is recorded — every batch gets a date, and sales
sell the oldest batch first. Turning it on later means your existing stock has no batch dates, so
decide before you load your opening stock. See
[Catch stock before it expires](./expiry-tracking.md).

Your subscription plan decides which features are available at all. Anything not in your plan shows
as locked, with a link to change your plan.
