# Set your cost prices

Profit is what you sold something for, minus what you paid for it. If EzyCore does not know what
you paid, it cannot show a profit — the dashboard says **Set cost prices to see profit** instead of
guessing. This page is where you tell it.

## The quick way: one category at a time

Most shops pay about the same for everything of one kind. So you rarely need to type a price for
every product.

1. Open **Products → Cost prices**.
2. Turn on **Only products without a cost**.
3. Pick a category, for example Floor Mats.
4. Type what you pay for one, and press **Apply**. Every product shown gets that cost.
5. Change the few that cost more or less.
6. Do the next category, then press **Save**.

The **Margin** column shows how much of the price you keep, so a wrong number stands out.

## What changes when you save

The new cost applies to **orders sent to the courier and sales made from now on**. Orders and sales
from before keep the cost they had — an old order without a cost stays "not costed", and the
dashboard says how much revenue that is.

So profit starts to appear from today, and fills in as new orders go out.

## Why the page is read-only

If you track stock and buy from suppliers in EzyCore, your cost price comes from your purchases: it
updates each time you receive stock. You cannot type over it here, because that would change the
value of stock already on your shelf.
