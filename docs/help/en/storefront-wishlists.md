# See what shoppers saved

When your shop's wishlist is on (Customize → Header → **Wishlist**), shoppers tap a heart to save a
product for later. **Wishlists** shows you what they saved. It counts shoppers with an account and
guests alike, but only names the ones with an account.

## The four numbers at the top

- **Wishlists** — how many shoppers have saved at least one product, split into signed-in and guest.
- **Products saved** — everything in every wishlist added together.
- **Saved recently** — products saved in the last 30 days.
- **Bought after saving** — saved products that were then ordered by the same shopper, in the last
  30 days.

## Most wished, and wished but sold out

**Most wished** lists the products saved by the most shoppers. A product high on this list but low
in your sales is worth a second look at its price or photos.

**Wished but sold out** is the restock list: people want these and nobody can buy them. Restock
from the top.

A guest's saves count here even though they have no account. A guest's list is kept on their own
phone or computer. Once a shopper signs in, their list is saved to their account and follows them
to any device.

## One customer's wishlist

Open a customer from **Customers → Online accounts** to see what they saved, when, and whether they
have since bought it.

## Wishlist alerts

The **Email shoppers about their wishlist** switch at the bottom of the page sends one email when
something a shopper saved is **back in stock**, or its **price drops**. Each kind has its own switch.

- It is **off** until you turn it on.
- Only shoppers who signed in **and** turned on **Wishlist alerts** in their account's notification
  settings get these emails. That setting starts off too, so nobody is emailed without asking.
  Guests cannot be emailed.
- The emails are checked every hour. A product sends once when it comes back, and a second price
  drop only sends if the price falls below the last one announced.
- Every alert email has an **unsubscribe** link, and order emails are never affected by it.
