# Simplify your forms

**Field Settings** lets you *Customize which fields appear in forms for your organization.*

EzyCore serves pharmacies, grocers, clothing shops and hardware stores, so its forms carry fields no
single business needs all of. Hiding the ones you never fill in makes every form shorter and gives
your staff fewer chances to enter something wrong.

## How it works

Pick the form you want to change, then *Toggle the switch to show or hide fields in the form.* A
preview shows what your staff will see. Hidden fields appear as badges, and clicking a badge brings
it back.

Changes apply to your whole organization, not just you.

## What to hide

Go through the product form first — it is the longest and the one used most.

Reasonable to hide if you do not use them:

- Batch and expiry, unless you sell anything perishable
- Brand, if you sell unbranded goods
- Barcode, if you do not scan
- Tax fields, if you do not charge tax

**Required fields cannot be hidden.** If a field will not switch off, it is because the system needs
it to work.

## What hiding does not do

Hiding a field removes it from the form. It does not delete data already saved in it, and it does not
stop that data appearing in reports or exports.

So hiding is safe and reversible — but it also means a field you hide after months of use still holds
old values that your reports will keep showing. If the data is wrong rather than unwanted, correct it
before hiding the field, not after.

## A caution

Hide too much and you will find a field missing at the moment you need it — usually at the counter,
with a customer waiting. Hide what you are confident you will never fill in, and leave the rest.
