# Support access

When you report a problem, our support team sometimes needs to see your shop the way
you see it — the same screens, with your real numbers on them. Guessing from a screenshot
wastes your time and ours.

**Settings → Support access** is where you see every time that has happened, and where you
can stop it while it is happening.

## What support can and cannot do

They can **look**. That is the whole list.

Every screen you have, they can read. Nothing else:

| | |
|---|---|
| Change anything | **No.** Not products, not prices, not stock, not orders. Every save button refuses. |
| Download your customer list | **No.** Bulk export is blocked. |
| See passwords or security settings | **No.** Nobody can, support included. |
| See your payment gateway keys | **No.** |
| Place an order or take a payment as you | **No.** |

If something genuinely needs changing, support will walk you through it while looking at the
same screen. There is no "let me just do it for you" — by design.

## How long it lasts

**Thirty minutes**, and it stops on its own. There is no way to extend it. If they need
longer, they open a new session, which means stating a new reason that you will see.

## How you know

While support is in your shop, a **banner sits across the top of every page**, naming the
person and why they are there. You cannot miss it, and it does not go away until the session
does.

## How to stop it

Click **End session** — either on the banner, or on this page. It takes effect immediately;
the next thing they click, they are locked out.

You never have to explain why. It is your shop.

## The record

Every session stays on this page permanently, showing:

- **who** at EzyCore opened it (their email address)
- **why** — the reason they typed before it started, word for word
- **when** it started, and how long it lasted
- **how many pages** they looked at
- **who ended it** — you, them, or the clock

We keep the reason exactly as it was written. If it does not match what you were told on the
phone, that is worth asking us about.

## Questions people ask

**Can I turn this off completely?**
Not today. What we promise instead is that access is read-only, time-limited, always visible
while it happens, permanently recorded, and stoppable by you at any moment. If you would
rather approve each session in advance, tell us — it is a change we are willing to make.

**Does support need my password?**
Never. They cannot log in as you and there is nothing to share. Nobody at EzyCore should ever
ask you for your password — if someone does, that is not us.

**Will they see my customers' details?**
They can read the same customer records you can, because that is often what the problem is
about. They cannot download or export them.

**It says "never opened" next to a session. What does that mean?**
Someone started a session but never actually opened your shop — perhaps the ticket was solved
first. Nobody looked at anything.
