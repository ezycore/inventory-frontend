# Invite your team

Give every member of staff their own account. Sharing one login costs you the ability to tell who
did what — and Stock History, sales records and adjustment reasons all become far less useful when
every action is attributed to the same person.

## Add a user

Invite staff by email from the Users screen. They receive an invitation, set their own password, and
sign in with your workspace slug.

Each user gets a role, which decides what they can see and do.

## Roles

A role is a named set of permissions. Rather than granting permissions person by person, you define
roles once — Cashier, Manager, Stock Keeper — and assign people to them. Change the role and everyone
holding it changes with it.

Permissions cover viewing, creating, editing and deleting for each area of the app. A role with no
permission for an area does not see it in the menu at all.

## A sensible starting point

| Role | Typically can | Typically cannot |
|---|---|---|
| Cashier | Make sales, look up products and customers | See cost prices, profit, or reports |
| Stock Keeper | Record purchases, adjust and transfer stock | Make sales, manage users |
| Manager | Everything operational, all reports, billing | Delete the organization |
| Owner | Everything | — |

The permission worth thinking hardest about is **cost price**. It appears in purchases, stock
valuation and profit reports, and most owners would rather counter staff could not see their margins.

## Removing someone

Deactivate the user rather than deleting them. Their access stops immediately, but their history
stays attached to their name — which is the whole point of separate accounts.
