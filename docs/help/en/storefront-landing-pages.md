# Build a landing page for an ad

A landing page is one page on your store, made for one offer — an Eid sale, a new arrival, the page a
Facebook ad sends people to. You build it from sections and it gets its own address, like
`yourstore.com/pages/eid-offer`.

## A landing page or your homepage?

Decide this first.

- **Changing how your whole store looks** — the homepage, the menu, how every product card looks —
  is done in **Customize**. See [Put your products online](./storefront-catalog.md).
- **A page for one campaign** that you point an ad or a message at is a landing page. Make it under
  **Pages**.

A landing page does not change your homepage, and shoppers only find it through the link you share.

## Make a page

1. Open **Pages** and press **New landing page**.
2. Give it a **Page name** you will recognise, like "Eid offer". Shoppers do not see this name.
3. Press **Create and open editor**.

The page starts empty and hidden. Nobody can open it until you publish it.

## Add and arrange sections

The list on the left is your page, top to bottom. Press **Add section** and pick one — a **Hero**
banner, a **Product grid**, **Selected products**, an **FAQ**, a **Call to action** and more. It goes
below the section you have open, or at the end.

Each row has buttons to **Move up**, **Move down**, **Hide section**, **Duplicate section** and
**Remove section**. A hidden section stays in the editor but shoppers do not see it.

Press a section's name, or click it in the preview, to change its settings. Press **Back to sections**
to return to the list.

A row marked **Unfinished — not saved yet** is missing something it needs, such as a picture or the
products to show. Fill it in; until then it is not saved and will not be published.

### Phones and computers

Under **Show on**, turn a section off for **Phones** or for **Computers and tablets** — a wide banner
that crowds a small screen, for example.

Some layout settings — **Columns**, **Alignment**, **Picture height (px)** and a few others — can
be set separately for phones. They are marked **Can differ on phones**. Switch the preview to
**Mobile view** and they read **Same as desktop**; change one and phones get their own value.
**Reset to desktop** puts it back.

### Product photos

Product sections have a **Card photo shape** (Square, Portrait, Landscape, Extra tall) and a
**Card photo fit** (Full photo shows the whole picture, Cropped fills the card). Leave them on
**Default** to match every other product card on your store. Change them when this page's products
look better in another shape — tall photos for sarees, wide ones for furniture.

Pictures you add to a section can be up to 5 MB.

## Saving

You do not need to press save. Your changes save by themselves a moment after you stop typing. The
top of the editor tells you where things stand: **Unsaved changes**, **Saving…** or **Draft saved**.

Press **Undo** or **Redo** to step back and forward, or use Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Shift+Z (⌘Z and ⌘⇧Z on a
Mac).

The preview on the right shows the page as it is right now, including changes not yet saved. Switch
between **Desktop view** and **Mobile view** to check both.

## Publish

Press **Publish**. The page is now live at its address, and **Pages** shows a **View** link to it
under **Live page**. Copy that address into your ad.

After it is live, keep editing as before. Shoppers still see the published version until you press
**Publish changes**. The list of pages marks a page like this with **Unpublished changes**.

- **Discard changes** throws away everything changed since the last publish and puts the editor back
  to what is live.
- **Unpublish** takes the page down. Shoppers who open its address get a page-not-found. Your content
  stays, and **Turn back on** puts it back up.

### Go back to an earlier version

Press **History**. Every publish is kept, up to the last 20, and the one shoppers see now is marked
**Live**. Press **Restore** on a version to bring it back into the editor. Nothing changes for shoppers
until you publish it.

## Page settings

Press **Page settings** to change:

- **Page name** — for you only.
- **Address** — the part after `/pages/`. Letters, numbers and dashes. If the page was already
  published, links to the old address keep working and lead to the new one, so an ad that is already
  running does not break.
- **Header and footer** — **Full store header and footer** gives shoppers your menu, search and cart.
  **Logo only** keeps them on the offer, with one way back to your store. **No header or footer**
  shows nothing but your sections.
- **Search engines** — the **Title** and **Description** Google shows. **Hide from search engines** is
  on for every new page, because an ad page is usually not worth finding by search. Turn it off for a
  page you want people to find on their own.

These save when you press **Save**, and take effect straight away, even on a published page.

## See the orders a page brought in

In **Pages**, the **Orders** column counts the orders placed by shoppers who came through each page —
including a shopper who looked at another product first and ordered from the normal checkout. Press the
number to open just those orders in your order list.

An order counts for a page only when it is placed on the same visit, in the same browser. A shopper who
comes back days later without opening the page again is not counted for it.

## Copy or delete a page

In **Pages**, **Duplicate** makes a copy to start the next campaign from. **Delete page** removes it
for good, and its address stops working.

## Why shoppers do not see my change

Check these in order:

1. **It is not published.** The editor says **Draft saved**, but that is your draft. Press
   **Publish changes**.
2. **The section is hidden.** Its row in the list says **Hidden from shoppers**.
3. **It is off for that screen.** Open the section and look under **Show on** — you may be checking on a
   phone with **Phones** turned off.
4. **The section is unfinished.** A row marked **Unfinished — not saved yet** is never published.
5. **The page was unpublished.** The button reads **Turn back on**.

If the editor shows *This page was saved somewhere else*, the page is open in another tab or on
another device. Press **Reload** and carry on from what was saved — changes made in this tab after the
last save are lost. Keep each page open in one place at a time.
